Formula

offer = guaranteed hours × hourly rate + AEP + travel
Δ £ = new − old
Δ % = (new − old) ÷ old

Notes

  • Travel is not calculated here: it is the median of the travel pay lines actually offered for jobs in each town, from one fixed home base, so it reflects the agency’s own distance for each venue (Doncaster offers ranged £12.04–£14.12, Sheffield £10.80–£14.16). An interpreter based elsewhere will see different travel figures but the same Δ £.
  • Travel is unchanged. For every town listed the old and new travel rules produce the same amount (the caps differ only beyond 200 miles one way).
  • Class mapping follows the new Qualification Framework: first, preliminary and plea hearings are Community; trials, evidence, sentencing, appeals, substantive tribunals, children’s cases, case management after the first hearing and legal argument are Professional.
  • Not included: urgency, out-of-hours and security uplifts, and the new “early acceptance” reward, whose value has not been published.
  • Amounts in pounds sterling, rounded to the penny.
